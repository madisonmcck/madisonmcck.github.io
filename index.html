The through line

One of my earliest initiatives focused on using public art, social media, and global networks to drive action on refugee rights and access to education.

From there I moved toward the levers that turn individual interventions into systems-level change: global policy, philanthropy, and talent development. At Global Citizen I ran the education and refugee-rights advocacy portfolio, securing more than $265M in commitments from HP, Gucci, and the governments of Denmark, the UAE, Tanzania, and Antigua and Barbuda.

At Comic Relief US I directed a $350M portfolio reaching 35 million children. I founded the Youth Advisory Council and the Juntanza Fund, both of which have outlasted my tenure by four years. At Schmidt Futures I led Rise, a global fellowship expanding educational and entrepreneurial pathways for young people.

Over the last three years that question brought me to AI. The economic mobility field is still early in its AI journey, and organizations are looking for leaders who can help them navigate the transition. I have seen where models work, where the ecosystem has friction, what talent developers need, and what it takes to move a partnership from surface-level adoption into deployment with real outcomes.

I want to spend the next chapter scaling that trust and turning what works into replicable infrastructure the broader workforce field can use.

Centering Technology in Systems Level Change

AI may be one of the most consequential tools we have for expanding human agency and economic opportunity. Its benefits will depend enormously on how it gets deployed and who gets access to it.

On why I moved into this work

What I'm doing now

I scoped CodePath's partnership with Anthropic and grew it into the largest strategic and revenue relationship in the organization's history in six months, then secured its renewal through 2027. The work spans research, classroom adoption, Claude Corps, the Knowledge Network, and an organization-wide shift to working AI-first.

We reached 100% classroom and staff adoption of Claude for 20,000 students in the Applied AI Pathway, and partnered with our CTO to return hundreds of hours to operations and student teams. That shift belongs to the whole team.

As of July 2026 I'm building ventures from zero to one: the strategy, funding, partnerships, talent, and economics required to launch and scale, plus the shape of CodePath's broader venture portfolio.
